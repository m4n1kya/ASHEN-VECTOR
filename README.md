\# ASHEN VECTOR



\### Mathematical Quantitative Research Engine



ASHEN VECTOR is a quantitative research platform focused on discovering,

testing, and evaluating mathematical relationships in financial markets.



The system emphasizes:



\- Mathematical factor construction

\- Statistical alpha discovery

\- Signal generation

\- Portfolio construction

\- Risk modeling

\- Systematic backtesting

\- Out-of-sample validation



ASHEN VECTOR does not rely on generative AI to directly predict stock prices.



\## Architecture



Market Data

→ Mathematical Factors

→ Alpha Discovery

→ Signal Generation

→ Portfolio Construction

→ Risk Management

→ Backtesting

→ Evaluation



\## Research Philosophy



> Extract signal from noise through mathematics, statistics, and systematic experimentation.



\## Status



Research platform — early development.
